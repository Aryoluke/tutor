First Session

What to expect

Nervous about the first session? Totally normal. Here's exactly what happens so you feel prepared.

Before the session
You're already ahead

Here's what's already taken care of before we even meet.

🎬

We've already chatted about your goals

I already know what you're looking for — whether it's maths help, gym coaching, or both. No need to explain yourself again.

📍

You know when and where

We've agreed on a time and place (or a video call link). You've got everything you need to show up, and that's it.

🎒

Just bring yourself

For maths: pen, paper, calculator if you have one. For gym: comfortable clothes and water. Other than that, just you.

The first 10 minutes
Just a relaxed chat

I'll start by asking a few questions about what you're finding hard — nothing formal, just getting to know where you're at. There are no tests, no quizzes, and no judgment. Think of it like telling a friend what's been tricky. This helps me figure out the best place to start, and it helps you feel comfortable working with me.

The session itself
We work at your pace

What we actually do depends on whether you're here for maths, gym, or both.

📐

Maths sessions

We'll pick a topic you're stuck on and work through it together — step by step. I explain, you try, we figure out what clicks. No rushing, no skipping ahead.

🏋️‍♂️

Gym sessions

We start with a form assessment and a light workout. I'll check your movement patterns, go through the basics, and build a plan that's safe and effective for your level.

After the session
What happens next

Before you leave, I'll give you a quick recap of what we covered and what I noticed. I'll suggest a couple of things you can practise on your own if you want to. Then I'll ask if you'd like to book another session — no pressure either way. Some people book their next one straight away, others take time to think about it. Both are fine.

Common worries
Things people worry about (you're not alone)

Almost everyone I work with has felt at least one of these. Here's the honest answer.

"What if I don't know anything?"

That's the whole point of working together. I don't expect you to know things — I expect you to be willing to learn. We start from wherever you're at, even if that's square one.

"What if I'm really behind?"

There's no such thing as too far behind. I've worked with students who were months behind and caught up fast. Small steps, consistent effort — it works every time.

"What if I'm too unfit?"

The gym is for getting fitter, not for people who are already there. Every exercise I program can be scaled to your level. You won't be asked to do anything you're not ready for.

"What if we don't click?"

It happens sometimes, and that's okay. If you feel we're not a good fit, just say so — no hard feelings. I can even point you toward someone who might be a better match.

Ready to book your first session?

No pressure, no obligation. Just a conversation to see if we'd work well together.
