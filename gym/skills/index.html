Prerequisites — be honest with yourself

Muscle-Up: 8–10 clean pull-ups + 8–10 straight bar dips

If you can't do these yet, we'll build them first. Skipping prerequisites is how people get injured and stuck.

Front Lever: solid L-sit hold + 10 pull-ups

The front lever puts huge demand on your lats and core. We need a strong foundation before attempting progressions.

Planche: 15+ clean push-ups + 30s plank with proper form

Wrist health is critical here. We build tendon strength slowly — rushing this leads to injury that can set you back months.

Handstand: basic shoulder mobility + comfort being upside down

No strict strength requirement, but being comfortable kicking up against a wall is step one.

How I approach skills

Assessment

We test where you're at — current strength, mobility, and any weak points that need addressing before the skill work begins.

Progression mapping

I'll map out the exact steps from where you are to where you want to be. Each step has a clear checkpoint so you know when to move on.

Technique coaching

Most people plateau because of bad technique, not lack of strength. I'll film your attempts and break down exactly what to fix.

Accessory work

The exercises most people skip — wrist prep, scapula work, rotator cuff strengthening. These prevent injury and unlock faster progress.
