Be honest with yourself
Prerequisites
Skipping prerequisites is how people get injured and stuck. Here's what you should have before starting each skill.

Muscle-Up: 8–10 clean pull-ups + 8–10 straight bar dips

If you can't do these yet, we'll build them first. The transition demands explosive pulling power and dip strength working together.

Front Lever: solid L-sit hold + 10 pull-ups

The front lever puts huge demand on your lats and core. We need a strong foundation before attempting any progressions.

Planche: 15+ clean push-ups + 30s plank with proper form

Wrist health is critical here. We build tendon strength slowly — rushing leads to injury that can set you back months.

Handstand: basic shoulder mobility + comfort being upside down

No strict strength requirement, but being comfortable kicking up against a wall is step one. We work on body alignment from day one.

My method
How I approach skills
Skills training is more than just drilling the move. Here's the full system I use to get you there safely and efficiently.

Assessment

We test where you're at — current strength, mobility, and any weak points that need addressing before the skill work begins. No assumptions.

Progression mapping

I'll map out the exact steps from where you are to where you want to be. Each step has a clear checkpoint so you know when to move on.

Technique coaching

Most people plateau because of bad technique, not lack of strength. I'll film your attempts and break down exactly what to fix.

Accessory work

The exercises most people skip — wrist prep, scapula work, rotator cuff strengthening. These prevent injury and unlock faster progress.
