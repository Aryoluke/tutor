POSTURE CORRECTION

Fix rounded shoulders & APT

Years of sitting, gaming, and studying take a toll on your posture. I'll show you how to fix it with simple, targeted exercises.

Common issues I see

Rounded Shoulders

Forward shoulder position from hunching over desks and screens. Causes tight chest muscles and weak upper back. Makes you look slouched and can lead to shoulder pain.

Anterior Pelvic Tilt (APT)

Excessive forward tilt of the pelvis caused by prolonged sitting and weak glutes/core. Creates a "duck-like" posture and can cause lower back pain.

Forward Head Posture

Head jutting forward from looking at screens. Puts immense stress on the neck and upper spine. Can cause headaches, neck pain, and reduced mobility.

The fix

1

Assessment

Identify your specific imbalances through simple posture checks and mobility tests. Know what you're working on.

2

Daily exercises (10-15 mins)

A short daily routine targeting your specific issues. Consistency beats intensity for posture correction.

3

Strengthening weak muscles

Strengthen the muscles that pull you into good posture: upper back, glutes, core, and deep neck flexors.

4

Stretching tight ones

Stretch the muscles pulling you out of alignment: chest, hip flexors, hamstrings, and upper traps.

Sample routine

Do this daily — takes about 12 minutes:

  • Chin tucks — 3 sets of 10, hold 5 secs
  • Wall angles — 3 sets of 10
  • Doorway stretch — 3 sets of 30 secs per side
  • Glute bridges — 3 sets of 15
  • Dead hangs — 3 sets of 20-30 secs
