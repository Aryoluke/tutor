Training philosophy
How I think about fitness
No shortcuts. No gimmicks. Just smart, consistent work that builds real results.

Form before weight

Adding weight to bad form multiplies injury risk. We lock in technique first — the strength follows naturally.

Progressive overload

Getting stronger week by week. Not by maxing out every session, but by small, sustainable increases in volume or intensity.

Recovery is training

You don't build muscle during the workout — you build it during recovery. Sleep, nutrition, and rest days are non-negotiable.

Consistency beats everything

A mediocre programme done consistently beats a perfect programme done occasionally. Show up, do the work, trust the process.
