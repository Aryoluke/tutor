Sample home workouts

Full Body — Beginner

3 rounds · 90s rest between rounds · 25 mins
  • Knee push-ups — 8–12 reps
  • Dead hangs — 20–30 seconds
  • Bodyweight squats — 12–15 reps
  • Plank hold — 30 seconds
  • Glute bridges — 12 reps

Full Body — Intermediate

3 rounds · 90s rest between rounds · 35 mins
  • Push-ups — 12–20 reps
  • Pull-ups — 5–10 reps (band-assisted if needed)
  • Bulgarian split squats — 10 per leg
  • Hanging knee raises — 8–12 reps
  • Pike push-ups — 8–12 reps

Push Day — Advanced

4 rounds · 2 min rest · 40 mins
  • Weighted dips (backpack) — 8–10 reps
  • Pseudo planche push-ups — 8–12 reps
  • Archer push-ups — 6–8 per side
  • Tricep extensions (band) — 12–15 reps
  • Lateral raises (band) — 15–20 reps

Pull Day — Advanced

4 rounds · 2 min rest · 40 mins
  • Weighted pull-ups (backpack) — 5–8 reps
  • Bodyweight rows (table/bar) — 10–15 reps
  • Face pulls (band) — 15–20 reps
  • Hammer curls (band) — 12–15 per arm
  • Dead hangs — max hold x 3

How I programme home workouts

Progressive overload at home

Without weights, we use volume, tempo, leverage, and band resistance to keep getting stronger. Adding one rep per session compounds fast.

Rucking & weighted carries

A backpack with books or water bottles is a legit training tool. Weighted walks build endurance and leg strength with zero equipment cost.

Minimalist splits

Push/pull/legs works perfectly at home. Upper/lower splits too. I'll design a weekly structure that fits your space and schedule.

Form checks via video

If we're working online, you send me clips of your sets and I'll give detailed feedback. Same quality coaching, just remote.
