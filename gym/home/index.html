Home Workouts

No gym, no problem

Effective training with minimal equipment. Dumbbells, a pull-up bar, or just your bodyweight — I'll build a plan that works wherever you are.

Equipment you need

Start with what you have

You don't need a full gym. Here's what makes home workouts effective.

Pull-up bar

The single best investment. Doorframe bar works perfectly. Essential for back strength and unlocking calisthenics skills.

Adjustable dumbbells

Versatile and space-efficient. Covers everything from curls to goblet squats to shoulder work.

Paralettes

Small, cheap, game-changing. Perfect for push-up progressions, L-sits, and handstand work.

Resistance bands

Warm-ups, assisted pull-ups, banded push-ups. The most versatile piece of kit under a tenner.

Sample workouts

What a session looks like

Upper body push

Diamond push-ups, pike push-ups, dumbbell press, tricep dips. 4 sets each, 60s rest.

Upper body pull

Pull-ups, chin-ups, inverted rows, banded face pulls. 4 sets each. Progressive overload every week.

Legs + core

Goblet squats, Bulgarian split squats, hanging leg raises, planks. Build dense leg strength without a squat rack.

Full body

Burpees, dumbbell thrusters, pull-ups, L-sit holds. 3 rounds for time. Efficient when you're short on time.

Skill work

Muscle-up transitions, front lever progressions, handstand holds. Dedicated skill practice to unlock advanced moves.

Mobility + recovery

Active stretching, banded distractions, foam rolling. Keeps you injury-free and moving well between hard sessions.

Pro tips

Getting the most from home training

Train at the same time daily

Routine is everything. Pick a time and protect it like an appointment you can't miss.

Track everything

Reps, sets, weight. If you're not tracking, you're guessing. Progressive overload needs data.

Film your sets

You can't see your own form. Record your top sets and check technique between rest periods.

Warm up properly

5-10 mins of dynamic stretching and light movement before every session. Your joints will thank you.
