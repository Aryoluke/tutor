Equipment guide

Six essentials. Most of them cost next to nothing — or nothing at all.

🏋️

Pull-up bar

~£20

Doorframe or wall-mounted. The single best piece of home gym kit you can own.

💪

Parallettes

~£25

Low push-up bars that save your wrists and unlock full ROM pressing.

🎒

Backpack + books

Free

Fill a rucksack with textbooks for instant, adjustable weight — squats, carries, rows.

🔴

Resistance bands

~£10

Loop bands for pull-up assistance, glute work, and banded push-ups. Compact and versatile.

🧘

Floor space

Free

2m × 2m of clear floor is all you need for lunges, planks, bridges and mobility work.

🪑

Chair or table

Free

For Bulgarian split squats, incline push-ups, and box step-ups. Your furniture is gym equipment.

How I programme

The principles behind every workout I write — whether you're in a garage, a bedroom, or a hotel room.

📈

Progressive overload at home

Add reps, reduce rest, increase time under tension, or switch to harder variations. The bar still moves up — even without a barbell.

🎒

Rucking & weighted carries

Load a backpack and walk. Then add lunges, step-ups, and carries. It's boring. It works. Cardio that doesn't kill gains.

✂️

Minimalist splits

Push / Pull / Legs fits home training perfectly. Two compound lifts plus accessories. 45 minutes, door to door, done.

📱

Form checks via video

Film a set, watch it back, fix one thing. No coach needed — just honest eyes and a phone tripod. Progress compounds fast.
