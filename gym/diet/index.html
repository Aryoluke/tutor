Practical tips

01

Protein at every meal

Distribute protein across 3-4 meals rather than one big serving. Your muscles use it better and you stay fuller longer.

02

Water before anything else

Drink a glass of water first thing and before every meal. Thirst is often mistaken for hunger - hydrate before you eat.

03

Eat the same breakfast

A consistent breakfast removes decision fatigue. Find one balanced meal that works and repeat. Save your willpower for later.

04

Don't cut out foods you love

Restriction leads to bingeing. Fit your favourites into your macros - 80% nutritious, 20% fun. Sustainable, not perfect.

05

Sleep is nutrition too

Poor sleep increases cravings and messes with hunger hormones. Prioritise 7-9 hours - it's a force multiplier for your diet.

06

Track for 2 weeks then relax

Track everything for 14 days to build awareness. After that, you'll know portion sizes by sight - no app needed.
