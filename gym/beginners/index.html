Beginner Calisthenics

Start from zero. Build real strength.

Everyone starts somewhere

Can't do a pull-up? Never touched a pull-up bar? That's completely normal. Most people I coach started exactly there. I'll show you the path from zero to your first rep — safely and with proper form.

The beginner path

1

Assessment & baseline

We figure out where you're at right now — how you move, what feels weak, what feels tight. No judgement. This is your starting point and that's completely fine.

2

Foundation exercises

We start with the regressions that match your level: dead hangs, scapular pulls, negative pull-ups, knee push-ups, and core holds. Building the base before adding difficulty.

3

Form mastery

Before increasing reps or weight, we lock in proper form. I'll watch every movement and correct anything that could lead to injury. Good form now means faster progress later.

4

First milestones

Your first full push-up. Your first band-assisted pull-up. Your first unassisted pull-up. We celebrate every win — because they're genuinely earned.

5

Building consistency

Once the basics are solid, we build a sustainable routine. A simple programme you can follow on your own, with check-ins to track progress and adjust as you get stronger.

Common beginner concerns

"I'm too weak"

That's why you're here. We'll start with exercises you CAN do and build from there. Everyone was a beginner once.

"I don't have equipment"

Floor space and a doorway pull-up bar is plenty to start. I can recommend affordable options if you want to set up at home.

"I'm not fit enough"

No minimum fitness required. The whole point is to help you get there. Sessions are paced around what you can handle.

"I'll look stupid"

You won't. Sessions are one-to-one, private, and judgement-free. No mirrors, no audience, no ego.

"I have bad posture"

That's actually one of the most common reasons people come to me. We'll work on it as part of your programme — it's not a barrier to starting.

"I don't know where to start"

That's exactly what I'm here for. You don't need to figure it out alone. That's literally my job.

What you'll learn in the first month

Push movements

Push-up progressions matched to your level. From wall push-ups to knee push-ups to full push-ups. Proper hand placement, elbow angle, and core engagement.

Pull movements

Dead hangs → scapular pulls → negative pull-ups → band-assisted pull-ups. The progression that actually works for getting your first pull-up.

Core strength

Planks, hollow body holds, leg raises, dead bugs. Building the foundation that supports everything else you do.

Movement patterns

Squats, lunges, hinge movements. Learning to move well so you can build strength without building bad habits.

Ready to start?

No experience required. Just bring yourself and a willingness to learn.
