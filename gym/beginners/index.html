Common concerns

We've heard it all. Here's why none of it should stop you.

"I'm too weak"

Everyone starts somewhere. The program is designed for people who can't do a single push-up. That's the whole point — you'll build strength from exactly where you are.

"I don't have equipment"

All you need is a pull-up bar and some floor space. Most exercises use just your bodyweight. No dumbbells, no machines, no expensive gear.

"I'm not fit enough"

Calisthenics is about getting fit, not being fit already. The progressions start at a level anyone can handle and ramp up gradually.

"I'll look stupid"

Every single advanced athlete was once a beginner. Nobody is watching — they're all focused on their own workout. Your effort is what counts.

"I have bad posture"

Calisthenics is one of the best ways to fix posture. Dead hangs decompress the spine, rows strengthen the back, and core work builds stability.

"I don't know where to start"

That's exactly what this program is for. Follow the steps in order, and you'll never have to wonder what to do next.
