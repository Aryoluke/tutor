FAQ

Quick answers to common questions. If you don't see yours here, just ask.

General

Where are you based?
Stanmore, North London. I prefer in-person sessions locally but also offer online tutoring if that works better for you.
How long is a typical session?
One hour is standard. I've found it's the sweet spot — long enough to make real progress, short enough to stay focused.
Do I need to commit to a block of sessions?
Nope. You can book one session, see how it goes, and decide from there. If you want to book a bundle to save, that's an option too.
Can I do both maths and gym with you?
Yeah, loads of students do both. We can even mix sessions — maths one week, gym the next. Whatever fits your schedule.

Maths

I'm really behind in maths. Can you still help?
Absolutely. Being behind just means we need to find the gaps. We'll go back to wherever things stopped making sense and build up from there. No judgement.
Do you cover specific exam boards?
I focus on the content, not the board — but I know the differences between Edexcel, AQA, and OCR. Past papers from your specific board are part of exam prep.
What if I just need help with one topic?
That's fine. One-off sessions for a specific topic or exam paper are totally okay. No need to commit to a full programme.

Gym

I've never worked out before. Is that okay?
Completely fine. Most people I coach start from zero. We'll go at your pace and build good habits from day one.
Do I need a gym membership?
Not necessarily. A pull-up bar and some floor space is enough to start. If you have gym access, great — I can programme for that too.
Can you help with injuries or bad posture?
I can help with common posture issues like rounded shoulders and anterior pelvic tilt. For injuries or medical conditions, you should see a physio first. I'm happy to work alongside their advice.

Booking & payment

How do I pay?
Cash or bank transfer — whatever's easiest. Payment is after the session, not before.
What if I need to cancel?
Just give me a heads-up as early as you can. No cancellation fees — life happens. But please don't no-show without a message.
Do you tutor on weekends?
Yeah, weekends work. Just let me know what times you're thinking and we'll figure it out.

Still have questions?

Send me a message — happy to chat before you commit to anything.
