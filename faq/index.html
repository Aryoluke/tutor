FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers. If your question isn't here, just ask — I'm happy to chat before you commit to anything.

General
Where are you based?
Stanmore, North London. I prefer in-person sessions locally but also offer online tutoring if that works better for you. I can travel within reasonable distance.
How long is a typical session?
One hour is standard. I've found it's the sweet spot — long enough to make real progress, short enough to stay focused throughout.
Do I need to commit to a block of sessions?
Nope. You can book one session, see how it goes, and decide from there. If you want to book a 4-session bundle to save £10, that's an option — but there's no pressure.
Can I do both maths and gym with you?
Yeah, loads of students do both. We can mix sessions — maths one week, gym the next. Whatever fits your schedule and goals.
What ages do you teach?
Mainly secondary school and sixth form — roughly ages 11–18. For gym coaching, I'm happy to work with adults too. If you're outside that range, reach out and we can chat.
Maths
I'm really behind in maths. Can you still help?
Absolutely. Being behind just means we need to find the gaps and fill them. We'll go back to wherever things stopped making sense and build up from there. No judgement — I've seen this plenty of times.
Do you cover specific exam boards?
I focus on the content, not the board — but I know the differences between Edexcel, AQA, and OCR. Past papers from your specific board are a key part of exam prep. The maths is the same; the question style is what changes.
What if I just need help with one topic?
That's fine. One-off sessions for a specific topic or exam paper are totally okay. No need to commit to a full programme if you just need a hand with, say, trigonometry or differentiation.
Do you teach A-Level Further Maths?
I cover the standard A-Level (Pure, Mechanics, Statistics). For Further Maths topics, reach out and I'll let you know if I can help with what you need.
Gym
I've never worked out before. Is that okay?
Completely fine. Most people I coach start from zero or near it. We'll go at your pace and build good habits from day one. No expectations — just a willingness to learn.
Do I need a gym membership?
Not necessarily. A pull-up bar and some floor space is enough to start. If you have gym access, great — I can programme for that too. I'll work with whatever equipment you have available.
Can you help with injuries or bad posture?
I can help with common posture issues like rounded shoulders and anterior pelvic tilt. For injuries or diagnosed medical conditions, you should see a physio first. I'm happy to work alongside their advice and reinforce what they recommend.
What if I can't even do one push-up?
That's where most people start. We'll begin with knee push-ups or wall push-ups and progress from there. Every advanced move has a regression — we find yours and build up.
Booking & payment
How do I pay?
Cash or bank transfer — whatever's easiest. Payment is after the session, not before. I want you to feel comfortable with how things went.
What if I need to cancel?
Just give me a heads-up as early as you can. No cancellation fees — life happens and I get that. All I ask is that you don't no-show without a message.
Do you tutor on weekends?
Yeah, weekends work. Just let me know what times you're thinking and we'll figure it out. After school, evenings, and weekends are all available.
Where do sessions happen?
In-person sessions can be at my place, your place, or a public space like a library. For gym, we can use your home setup, a local park with bars, or a gym. Whatever you're comfortable with.
