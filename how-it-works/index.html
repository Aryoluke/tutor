How It Works

From first message to first session

Simple and no pressure. Here's exactly what happens when you reach out.

1

Reach out

Send me a message on WhatsApp or email. Tell me what you're looking for — maths, gym, or both — and roughly where you're at. No long forms. No commitment. Just a conversation starter.

2

Quick chat

We'll have a relaxed conversation about your goals, current level, and what kind of help would make the biggest difference. I'll ask a few questions to understand where you're at. This chat is free and obligation-free — it's just to make sure we're a good fit.

3

First session

We'll meet — in person or online — and start working on your goals. I'll assess where you're at, figure out a plan tailored to you, and we'll get straight into it. For maths, this might mean tackling a topic you're stuck on. For gym, it starts with a form and movement assessment. No judgement, just progress.

4

Keep going (or don't)

After the first session, you decide if you want to continue. No contracts, no pressure. If it's working for you, we'll set up regular sessions — weekly, fortnightly, whatever fits. If it's not what you expected, no hard feelings at all. You pay for what you use.

The fine print
