How it works

From first message to first session — simple and no pressure.
1

Reach out

Send me a message on WhatsApp or email. Tell me what you're looking for — maths, gym, or both — and roughly where you're at. No long forms, no commitment.

2

Quick chat

We'll have a relaxed conversation about your goals, current level, and what kind of help you need. This is free — it's just to make sure we're a good fit.

3

First session

We'll meet (in person or online) and start working on your goals. I'll assess where you're at, figure out a plan, and we'll get straight into it. No judgement, just progress.

4

Keep going (or don't)

After the first session, decide if you want to continue. No pressure, no contracts. If it's working, we'll set up regular sessions. If not, no hard feelings.

Ready to start?

Step 1 takes 30 seconds ¡ just send a message.
