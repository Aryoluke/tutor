For parents

Everything you need to know about how I work with your child.

Hi — I'm Aryan. I know choosing a tutor for your child is a big decision. You want someone who can actually help, not just go through the motions. Here's what you can expect from me.

I've recently been through GCSEs and A-Levels myself, so I understand the pressure students face — and I know how to make difficult topics click. I also know that confidence matters as much as content. My goal is for every student to leave a session feeling like they've made real progress.

Clear communication

After the first few sessions, I'll give you an honest update on where your child is at, what we're working on, and what progress looks like. No jargon, no fluff.

No long-term contracts

You pay per session. If it's not working out, you can stop anytime. I'd never want someone to feel locked in.

Flexible scheduling

After school, weekends, holidays — we'll find times that work around your family's schedule. I know how busy life gets.

Safe & professional

Sessions are either in your home (with a parent present) or in a public space like a library. Whatever you're comfortable with.

Real exam prep

For GCSE and A-Level students, we'll work through past papers together. I'll teach exam technique — how to read questions, manage time, and pick up marks.

Building confidence

Many students aren't struggling with the material — they're struggling with believing they can do it. I work on both.

Want to chat first?

I'm happy to hop on a quick call or exchange messages before you decide. No obligation.
