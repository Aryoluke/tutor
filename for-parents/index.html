For Parents

Everything you need to know

I know choosing a tutor for your child is a big decision. Here's what you can expect — honestly and clearly.

Hi — I'm Aryan. I've recently been through GCSEs and A-Levels myself, so I understand the pressure students face — and I know how to make difficult topics actually click.

I also know that confidence matters as much as content. Many students aren't struggling with the material — they're struggling with believing they can do it. I work on both.

I've been tutoring for a couple of years now, helping students go from failing to passing, from passing to thriving, and from hating maths to actually enjoying it. That's the part I care about most.

What to expect from me

Clear communication

After the first few sessions, I'll give you an honest update on where your child is at, what we're working on, and what progress looks like. No jargon, no fluff — just straightforward information.

No long-term contracts

You pay per session. Weekly, fortnightly, or as needed. If it's not working out — though I'll do everything I can to make sure it does — you can stop anytime with no penalty.

Flexible scheduling

After school, evenings, weekends, school holidays — we'll find times that work around your family's schedule. I know how busy life gets, especially during exam season.

Safe & professional

Sessions can be in your home with a parent present, in a public space like a library, or online. Whatever you're comfortable with. Safety and professionalism come first.

Real exam preparation

For GCSE and A-Level students, past papers are a core part of what we do. I'll teach exam technique — how to read questions properly, manage time in the exam hall, and pick up every available mark.

Building confidence

The biggest transformation I see isn't just grades — it's attitude. Students who used to dread maths start walking into sessions ready to learn. That shift is everything.
