From Grade 4 to Grade 7 in GCSE Maths

Year 11 · GCSE Maths

Starting point

Sarah was a Year 11 student predicted a grade 4. She struggled significantly with algebra — particularly solving equations and factorising — and had very little confidence in her maths ability. She often froze during tests and avoided attempting harder questions altogether.

What we did

Over 4 months of weekly sessions, we focused relentlessly on past papers and building genuine understanding rather than rote memorisation. We broke algebra down into small, manageable steps, and I gave her structured practice sheets that gradually increased in difficulty. Each session ended with a confidence-building recap of what she'd mastered.

The result

Sarah went from a grade 4 to a grade 7 — improving by three full grades. She described the exam as "actually enjoyable" and said she felt prepared for every question. Her confidence soared, and she started helping classmates with their algebra homework.

"I used to dread maths lessons. Now it's my favourite subject. Alex didn't just teach me maths — he taught me how to believe I could do it."— Sarah, Year 11

A-Level Maths: From Predicted D to B

Year 12 · A-Level Maths (Mechanics)

Starting point

James was a Year 12 student whose main struggle was mechanics. The application of forces, kinematics, and connected particles confused him, and he was panicking before every exam. He was predicted a D and was seriously considering dropping maths entirely.

What we did

Over 3 months of targeted sessions, we focused entirely on breaking down complex mechanics problems into simple, repeatable steps. We built a checklist approach: draw a diagram, label forces, resolve components, write equations, solve. I gave him exam-style problems each week and showed him how to spot patterns across different question types.

The result

James went from a predicted D to achieving a B in his end-of-year exams. He stopped panicking before tests and became methodical in his approach. He ended up actually enjoying mechanics — a subject he'd previously hated — and decided to keep maths for Year 13.

"I was so close to giving up. Alex showed me that mechanics wasn't impossible — I just needed the right framework. Getting that B felt incredible."— James, Year 12

Zero to Five Pull-Ups in 2 Months

Year 10 · Gym / Calisthenics

Starting point

Tom was a Year 10 student with zero fitness background. He spent most of his free time gaming and had developed noticeably poor posture — rounded shoulders and a forward head. He lacked energy, had no strength, and felt self-conscious in PE class.

What we did

Over 2 months of calisthenics coaching, we started from absolute basics: bodyweight rows, negative pull-ups, scapular pulls, and core stability work. We focused on form first and built strength progressively. Each 30-minute session was structured around 3–4 exercises, and we incorporated posture corrections throughout.

The result

Tom went from 0 to 5 strict pull-ups in just 2 months. His posture improved noticeably — his rounded shoulders flattened, his chest opened up, and he stood taller. His energy levels increased, he felt more confident in school, and he started going to the gym on his own. His parents reported he was "a completely different kid."

"I never thought I'd be able to do a single pull-up. Five feels insane. My posture is so much better and I actually look forward to exercising now."— Tom, Year 10
