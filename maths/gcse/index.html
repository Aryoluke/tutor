How sessions work

Topic teaching

We'll go through each topic methodically. I explain the concept, we do examples together, then you try with support. No moving on until it clicks.

Past paper practice

The single most effective way to prepare. We work through real exam papers, learn how to read questions, manage time, and pick up every available mark.

Mark scheme mastery

Understanding what examiners actually want is half the battle. I'll show you exactly how marks are awarded and the common pitfalls to avoid.

Exam technique

How to approach different question types, when to move on from a tough question, double-checking answers, and managing exam stress.

Which grade are you aiming for?

Grade 4–5 (Foundation)

For students who just need to pass. We focus on the core topics, building confidence, and making sure the basics are solid. No shame — everyone's journey is different.

Grade 6–7 (Higher)

For students aiming above a pass. We cover the full higher tier, develop problem-solving skills, and learn to tackle the harder questions without panicking.

Grade 8–9 (Higher)

For students targeting the top grades. We go deep on the most challenging topics, refine exam technique, and develop the level of understanding that separates an 8 from a 9.
