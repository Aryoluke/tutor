How I teach
Understanding over memorising
Most people don't struggle with maths — they struggle with how it was explained. My job is to make it clear.

Topic by topic

We work through each area methodically. I explain, we do examples together, then you try with support. No moving on until it clicks.

Past paper practice

The most effective way to prepare. We use real exam papers, learn how to read questions, manage time, and pick up every mark.

Exam technique

Understanding what examiners want is half the battle. I'll show you how marks are awarded and the common pitfalls to avoid.

Your pace, your path

Everyone learns differently. We'll adapt the approach to how you learn best — whether that's visual, step-by-step, or practice-heavy.
