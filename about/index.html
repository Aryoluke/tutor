About me

Hey, I'm Aryan. I'm based in Stanmore and I tutor maths and coach calisthenics. I started tutoring because I noticed how many people just needed things explained differently -- not faster, not louder, just clearer.

I've been through the full GCSE and A-Level maths journey myself, and I've helped other students through it too. I know where the common sticking points are because I've seen them -- and I've learned how to unstick them.

On the gym side, calisthenics has been a personal passion for years. I've made the mistakes, fixed my form, worked through plateaus, and learned what actually builds strength without getting hurt. Now I help others do the same.

How I work

Maths sessions

We start by figuring out where you're at -- what clicks and what doesn't. Then we work through topics step by step, with plenty of practice. Past papers, problem-solving strategies, and making sure you actually understand, not just memorise. I adapt to how you learn best.

Gym sessions

We begin with a form assessment -- how you move, what feels off, what your goals are. Then I build a programme around you. Every session we check technique, track progress, and adjust. No ego, no rushing, no exercises that don't serve a purpose.

What I believe

No judgement

Everyone starts somewhere. Being "behand" just means there's room to grow.

Understanding over memorising

If you get the why, the what becomes easy. I don't teach shortcuts.

Consistency beats intensity

Showing up regularly beats cramming. For both maths and gym.

Keep it simple

Most things aren't as complicated as they seem. My job is to make them clear.
