My story

From student to tutor

I've been through the full GCSE and A-Level maths journey, and I remember exactly what it's like to be stuck. Not the "'ll figure it out" kind of stuck — the "I've been staring at this for 20 minutes and I still don't get it" kind. That feeling is why I started tutoring.

Most people don't struggle with maths because they're bad at it. They struggle because someone explained it in a way that didn't click. A different explanation, a different approach, a bit more time — and suddenly it makes sense. That's what I do. I find the explanation that works for you.

On the gym side, calisthenics has been a personal obsession for years. I've made the mistakes — trained with bad form, hit plateaus, dealt with injuries, and learned what actually works. I've helped friends and students build real strength
