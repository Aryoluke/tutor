About

Hey, I'm Aryan

Based in Stanmore. I tutor maths and coach calisthenics. Here's why I do it and how I work.

My story
From student to tutor

I've been through the full GCSE and A-Level maths journey, and I remember exactly what it's like to be stuck. Not the "I'll figure it out" kind of stuck — the "I've been staring at this for 20 minutes and I still don't get it" kind. That feeling is why I started tutoring.

Most people don't struggle with maths because they're bad at it. They struggle because someone explained it in a way that didn't click. A different explanation, a different approach, a bit more time — and suddenly it makes sense. That's what I do. I find the explanation that works for you.

On the gym side, calisthenics has been a personal obsession for years. I've made the mistakes — trained with bad form, hit plateaus, dealt with injuries, and learned what actually works. I've helped friends and students build real strength without getting hurt. That's what I bring to every coaching session.

What I believe
The principles behind everything I do

No judgement. Ever.

Beeing behind, being weak, being confused — that's not a character flaw. It's just where you're at right now. We start there and move forward.

Understanding beats memorising

Every single time. If you understand why something works, you don't need to memorise it. This applies to maths AND gym.

Consistency over intensity

Showing up regularly beats going crazy once a week. For maths. For training. For everything. Steady progress is the only kind that sticks.

Keep it simple

Most things aren't as complicated as they seem. My job is to make them clear. No jargon, no overcomplicating, no showing off.

How sessions work
What to expect

We start by figuring out where you're at — what clicks and what doesn't. Then we work through topics step by step, with plenty of practice. Past papers, problem-solving strategies, and making sure you actually understand, not just memorise. I adapt to how you learn best, whether that's visual, step-by-step, or practice-heavy.
